# Important Limitations

Borrower Copilot is a self-assessment tool and does not provide a lender credit decision.

- Actual lender approval may differ.
- Actual interest rates may differ.
- Actual APR requires the lender's complete fee schedule.
- The collateral value used in the assessment is based on borrower-provided information.
- Unknown information widens estimates instead of being treated as zero.
- Future income from business expansion is not guaranteed and is not treated as guaranteed affordability.
