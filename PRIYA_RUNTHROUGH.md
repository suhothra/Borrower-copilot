# Assumptions and Limits

- This is a self-assessment tool, not a lender credit decision.
- Interest-rate bands are indicative assumptions.
- Actual lender pricing may differ.
- APR is estimated using the interest-rate range and an assumed processing fee.
- Exact APR requires the lender's complete fee and repayment schedule.
- Unknown information widens estimates rather than being treated as zero.
- Final loan eligibility depends on the lender's underwriting process.
